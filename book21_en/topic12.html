🍀 How Can Luck
Find You?

You cannot command luck, but you can increase the probability of being seen by opportunity and being ready when it arrives.

🎯 Luck Exists

Timing, judges, the economy, and who happens to see your work can all affect outcomes. Not everything is under personal control.

🚪 Waiting Is Not the Only Option

Accepting the role of luck does not mean becoming passive. You can increase how often you enter places where opportunities may happen.

🏆 Opportunity Can Arrive Suddenly

A speaking contest, an unexpected opening, or a person discovering your work may create a turning point without warning.

🧠 Preparation Decides What You Can Catch

When an opportunity appears, existing ability determines whether you can turn it into a real result.

👀 “They Were Just Lucky”

We often see only the final moment of success, not the preparation, failed attempts, and repetition that came before it.

🧲 Expand Your Luck Surface Area

Keep producing, join projects, show your work, and build trustworthy relationships. These actions increase the density of possible opportunities.

⚡ Effort Does Not Guarantee Luck

Effort cannot force a lucky event, but it can improve your ability to encounter, recognize, and use one.

🔎 Better Questions

Am I present where opportunities can actually occur?
If an opportunity appeared tomorrow, could my current ability handle it?
Can people easily see evidence of what I can do?
Can I increase the number of attempts without making each one too costly?

✨ Takeaway

You cannot control when luck arrives; you can control whether you are regularly in places where it can find you and ready enough to catch it.
